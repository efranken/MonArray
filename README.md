# what is?

A horizontal line array of 2" Tectonic BMRs.  4 left 4 right on outer sides for music, 1 left 1 right in middle for work computer.  Separate air chambers for each bank.  Wow.  All made with openscad from claue prompts.  Neat.  Fully Parameterized.  Great!

![The three pieces: box, backpanel (sectioned to show the wire channels), and trim](images/three_pieces.png)

The box, backpanel, and trim above are each cut into printable pieces for a 300mm-class printer bed. Regenerate `stl/` and this overview after a geometry change with `scripts/render.sh`:

![Every printable piece from stl/, laid out in a grid](images/pieces.png)

Designed for printing on a Creality K2 Pro or printer of similar buildplate size.
